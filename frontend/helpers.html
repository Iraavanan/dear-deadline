Helping someone with a Social Security letter

For librarians, senior-center and community staff, caseworkers, family and friends. You do not need to know Social Security rules to use this tool with someone.

Open the tool

How to use it with someone

  1. Start with the date on the letter. Choose what the letter says on the first screen and type in the date printed at the top. The deadlines appear straight away. If you do nothing else, write them on the letter.
  2. Let the person answer. The questions are theirs to answer, and "I don't know" is always an answer. Read the questions aloud if that helps.
  3. Use their words. Where the tool asks for their own words, type what they say. It goes into their draft exactly as written.
  4. Print the kit. Nothing is saved, so print or download the last page before closing the tab. The person files it themselves.

What to keep to

  • Never type a Social Security number, claim number or bank account number. The tool does not need them and blacks out any it finds in pasted text.
  • You are not giving legal advice, and neither is the tool. It shows dates and what a person can ask for. It does not say what they should choose or how a request will turn out.
  • Do not tell anyone a request is not worth making. The tool never does. If a deadline has passed, it shows how to ask for more time.
  • Close the tab when you finish on a shared computer. Closing it forgets everything.

When to send someone to legal aid

  • The amount is large, or the person cannot work out why they were overpaid.
  • A letter says disability benefits are ending. Part of that letter has a 15-day window and a choice with a cost.
  • They have already lost a reconsideration or a hearing.
  • Anything involving a court.

Legal aid offices help with Social Security letters, usually at no cost if income is low. Find legal aid. Social Security: 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778).

If you are not sure the letter is real

Use the warning-sign check first. Social Security will never ask for payment by gift card, wire transfer, cryptocurrency or cash, and will never tell anyone to keep a matter secret.
